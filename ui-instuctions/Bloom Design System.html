Design system v1 · 13 November 2026 · Android first

Bloom

A warm, calm, tactile system for a pregnancy app. Expressive in type, shape and motion where it matters, quiet everywhere else. One theme file, many languages.

4. Languages and localization

Every string lives in a resource file and no screen hard-codes text, so a language is added by translating strings and choosing a script font, not by redesigning screens. Which languages launch is a product decision. I would choose them from your user data, then add more in the same way.

Rules

  • Direction: use start/end, never left/right. Mirror layouts, nav order, progress bars and back arrows. Do not mirror numerals, the Bloom shape, or media icons.
  • Length: design for text 30–40% longer than English, with no fixed-width buttons.
  • Numbers and dates: format with the locale. Let the user choose the numeral style and calendar.
  • Plurals and gender: use ICU message formats. Offer neutral phrasing, since many languages gender “you”.
  • Language switch: in onboarding and in Me, and it applies instantly. Support the system per-app language setting.
  • Baby-size comparisons: a regional food set per language or region (for example mango, guava, pomegranate for South Asia).
  • Content: medical text is translated by a qualified reviewer, never machine-only.
  • Fallback: missing strings fall back to English and are flagged in testing.

6. Layout, targets and elevation

  • 4-point spacing grid. 20 px side margins on a 390 px screen.
  • Touch targets at least 48 dp with 8 dp between targets. Primary actions sit in the bottom 40% of the screen.
  • Bottom navigation: Today, Journal, Tools, Plan, each with icon and label. Profile sits behind an avatar button.
  • Elevation: soft tinted shadow (0 10 28, 10% season deep tone) on cards. Sheets and nav bars use a top shadow.

7. Components

ChipIron levelsScan date
  • Buttons: pill, 56 px high for primary and 48 px minimum otherwise. One primary per screen.
  • Chips: 20 px radius, 14/20 label. Used for symptoms, questions and labels.
  • Mood shapes: five shapes with text labels, 48 px, one tap, no score.
  • Cards: 28 px radius, white surface, one idea each. Only one Today card at a time.
  • Inputs: 56–60 px high, 2 px outline, label above, never placeholder-only.
  • Bottom sheet: 32 px top radius. Used for Premium and quick actions.
  • Progress segments: 4 px bars for onboarding and Week stories.
  • States: default, pressed (shape morph), focused (3 px outline), disabled (60% with a label), error (icon, words and #A3263A).

8. Motion and haptics

  • Spring motion, 200–400 ms for presses, selections and sheets. The Bloom morph is about 600 ms.
  • One slow ambient animation at most (the Bloom breathes). Everything else responds to the user.
  • Soft haptic on kick taps and contraction start and stop. Never for marketing.
  • Respect reduce-motion: use fades and stop ambient animation. No flashing. Sound is off by default.

9. Icons and illustration

  • Rounded 2 dp stroke icons at 24 dp, always with a text label in navigation.
  • Flat, grainy shapes. No faces, no skin tones, no gendered colors, no stock baby photos.
  • Local food illustrations for baby-size art, commissioned or licensed per region.

Never tell the user what a good result is for weight or kicks. Warning-sign text comes only from your medical reviewer.

11. Themes in use

  • Dawn: default. Season color follows the trimester.
  • Night: automatic, plus a one-tap switch on the contraction timer.
  • Quiet: forced for pause and loss. No upsell and no cross-promotion.

12. Accessibility checklist

  • Contrast 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for large text and icons, checked in every theme.
  • TalkBack labels on every icon button, logical focus order, moods announced by label.
  • Font scale 200% and large display size without clipping.
  • One-handed test with a thumb, and a night-time contraction task test.
